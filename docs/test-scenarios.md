# Deterministic operational scenarios

**Purpose:** the client confirmed the product is a single delayed-order
investigation workflow, not seven separate products. These seven orders
exist to verify that *the same investigation workflow* — gather context,
diagnose, recommend — correctly identifies a different root cause for each
one. They are not meant to exercise seven different feature sets; every
scenario is read through the same `OrderContextService` (and, later, the
same MCP tools) and should be distinguishable purely by the evidence it
returns.

These orders are hand-crafted directly in `src/data/orders.ts` (and the
corresponding `payments.ts`/`fulfillments.ts`/`inventory.ts` entries), not
part of the randomized bulk data, so their IDs and evidence stay stable.
They are referenced by `tests/services/order-context.service.test.ts` today,
and are intended to back the future `diagnose_order_delay` tool's test
coverage once the MCP is built.

| Order    | Scenario            | Payment  | Inventory              | Fulfillment          | Expected operational interpretation |
| -------- | ------------------- | -------- | ----------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| ORD-1001 | Normal               | paid     | available                | delivered              | No issue |
| ORD-1002 | Payment failure      | failed   | available (uninvolved)   | pending                | Payment problem |
| ORD-1003 | Inventory shortage   | paid     | unavailable (0 of 3)     | waiting_inventory      | Inventory problem |
| ORD-1004 | Fulfillment delay    | paid     | available                | processing (stalled)   | Fulfillment problem |
| ORD-1005 | Fulfillment failure  | paid     | available                | failed                 | Fulfillment problem |
| ORD-1006 | Cancelled            | refunded | available (released)     | cancelled              | Cancelled order |
| ORD-1007 | Partial inventory    | paid     | partial (2 of 5 needed)  | waiting_inventory      | Inventory problem |

## Notes per scenario

- **ORD-1001** — `C-001` orders 2× Wireless Mouse + 1× USB-C Cable. Payment
  paid, fulfillment delivered with a tracking number. No shortage signals
  anywhere in the composed context.
- **ORD-1002** — `C-002` orders 1× Mechanical Keyboard. Payment status is
  `failed`; order status is `failed`. Fulfillment never progressed past
  `pending` because it never received a paid order to act on.
- **ORD-1003** — `C-003` orders 3× Bluetooth Earbuds. Inventory for
  `P-004` shows `available: 0, reserved: 3` — fully out of stock relative to
  the 3 units needed. Fulfillment is `waiting_inventory`.
- **ORD-1004** — `C-004` orders 1× Laptop Stand + 2× Ceramic Mug. Both
  items have ample inventory. Fulfillment is stuck at `processing`, with
  `updatedAt` a full day after `order.createdAt` and no further progress —
  a fulfillment-side delay, not a stock problem.
- **ORD-1005** — `C-005` orders 1× Running Shoes. Inventory is available;
  fulfillment status is `failed` outright (e.g., failed handoff to carrier).
- **ORD-1006** — `C-006` orders 2× Fiction Novel, then cancels. Payment is
  `refunded`; fulfillment is `cancelled`; inventory shows no shortage
  (released back to stock).
- **ORD-1007** — `C-007` orders 5× Basmati Rice 5kg. Inventory for `P-016`
  shows `available: 2, reserved: 5` — enough for 2 of the 5 units ordered.
  Distinguishes a *partial* shortage from ORD-1003's *full* shortage.

## Scope note

These scenarios exercise investigation of a single order using payment,
inventory, and fulfillment as supporting evidence. They intentionally do
not include, and should not be extended to include, scenarios for
domain-specific problems unrelated to an order being delayed (e.g. "bulk
payment reconciliation" or "warehouse stock audit") — those would be
separate-domain workflows, which are explicitly out of scope. See
`docs/decisions.md`.

## Bulk (non-scenario) orders

`ORD-1008` through `ORD-1050` are generated by a seeded PRNG (see
`docs/decisions.md`) to provide surrounding "normal" traffic: a mix of
`completed`, `processing`, `pending`, `cancelled`, and `failed` orders with
internally consistent payment/fulfillment status derived from the order
status. They are not individually meaningful and are not asserted on by
name in tests — only the seven scenario orders above are.
